# Third-party asset policy

This classroom build contains a locally generated metadata catalog (names, National Pokédex numbers, generations, and evolution relationships), but does **not** redistribute Pokémon artwork.

Sprite images are requested from the PokeAPI sprite host only after a student enters through the authenticated classroom shell. The public static build stays locked without that classroom session. Do not add downloaded sprite files to this repository or distribute a packaged asset archive unless the school has separately verified that its permission covers that distribution.

Pokémon names, characters, and artwork belong to their respective owners. The application is intended for the single authorized classroom use described by the project owner. It is not an official Pokémon product and must not be advertised, sold, or used as a general public asset service.

The underlying Typer Kids code remains subject to its GPL-3.0 license. PokeAPI's code/data and sprite repository have their own notices; its sprite repository specifically notes that image contents are copyrighted by The Pokémon Company.
